Mater Dei — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Mater Dei is a Catholic prayer app for praying the Holy Rosary. It is built to be prayed with, not to gather data. This policy describes, completely, what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

Mater Dei collects no personal data. None. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no data is ever transmitted to us — we operate no servers.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or the app remembers is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device: prayer intentions and intention history; streaks and completed-Rosary counts; language, prayer, audio, and accessibility settings; the daily reminder time; and saved progress within a Rosary.

This information never leaves the app. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We operate no servers, so there is no copy for us to access, sell, or lose. On Android, automatic app backup is switched off deliberately so that none of it is copied to your Google Drive — the trade-off is that a new phone starts a fresh streak. On iOS, the app's data may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer), which is encrypted by Apple, controlled entirely by you, and never accessible to us.

Network use

The app makes network requests for exactly one purpose: streaming public-domain and freely licensed (Creative Commons) sacred media — Gregorian chant, a church bell, and the Mystery paintings — from Wikimedia Commons servers when a local copy is not bundled. These are ordinary web requests: like any web server, Wikimedia's servers see your IP address when serving the file. No intention, prayer, setting, or any other app data is included in these requests. Wikimedia's handling of server logs is governed by the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. Once cached, media plays offline without further requests.

Spoken prayer (text-to-speech)

Spoken prayer uses your device's own text-to-speech engine (provided by your operating system). Some system TTS voices are processed on-device; some, depending on your OS settings and the engine you have installed, may be processed by the engine vendor's servers. That behavior is controlled by your device's TTS settings, not by Mater Dei, and the app sends the engine only the public text of the prayers themselves.

Where a bundled recording of a prayer is available, it is played from the app itself and no text-to-speech engine is involved.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No notification service of ours is involved, because we don't have one.

Children

Mater Dei is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Permissions

The app requests only what its features need:

  • Notifications — the optional daily reminder.
  • Internet access — the Wikimedia streaming described above.
  • Run at startup — so a scheduled reminder survives a restart.
  • Vibration — the reminder's alert, and haptic feedback while praying.

It does not request the camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here with a new effective date before the change ships.
